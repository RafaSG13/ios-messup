Expense & payment tracking for the beautifully absent-minded.

Messup is a local-first iOS tracker for expenses, income and savings. SwiftUI with Liquid Glass, SwiftData on the device, on-device Apple Intelligence. No backend, no account, no third-party dependencies.

Features

Money picture

Week, month or year: spent, change vs the previous period at the same day, daily average, end-of-period projection, cumulative chart, category breakdown and up to 3 insights. Balance is income − expenses − contributions; one savings goal is funded by explicit contributions.

Budgets & alerts

A monthly limit plus optional per-category limits. Warning from 80 %, over above 100 %. Opt-in local notifications (once per level per month) and a weekly Sunday reminder.

Apple Intelligence

On device: quick add from text, photo or scanned receipt; category suggestion; streamed period summary; an “Ask Messup” chat that answers with tools over your data. Hidden when the model is unavailable.

Quick capture with Siri & Shortcuts

“Add an expense in Messup”: an App Intent with amount, name, category and payment method that shares the running app's services.

Private by design

SwiftData store on the device, no account, no backend, no analytics, no network calls.

Yours

Any ISO currency, CSV export, light and dark mode, Dynamic Type up to accessibility sizes.

Screens

How it works

Income is money in. The saving goal is funded only by explicit contributions, so goal progress is the sum of contributions and the balance is income − expenses − contributions. The monthly budget is ok below 80 %, warning from 80 % and over above 100 %; each level alerts once per month.

A sheet form awaits an async throws service method. The service writes records through LocalStore.context, saves, then reloads its observable array sorted by date, so every view re-renders. If the call throws, errorAlert shows the error and the sheet stays open.

Architecture

Model-View, no ViewModels. Protocol-typed @Observable services are injected with @Entry and shared with App Intents through AppServices.shared; the local and mock environments swap the implementations.

Apple Intelligence

Everything runs on the Foundation Models framework, on the device. The model is given computed figures and never asked to calculate, and every AI control is hidden unless availability == .available.

Theme

The mint accent is the only brand color and nothing in the UI is green: progress and “ok” use the accent, orange means approaching a limit, red means over a limit. Liquid Glass is used only on bars and controls, never on content cells.

Get started

  • Xcode 27 or later (iOS 27 SDK)
  • iOS 27.0+ device or simulator
  • Apple Intelligence features need an eligible device with Apple Intelligence on; otherwise they are hidden
git clone https://github.com/RafaSG13/ios-messup.git
cd ios-messup
open iOSMessup.xcodeproj

# build (get simulator ids from: xcrun simctl list devices available)
xcodebuild -project iOSMessup.xcodeproj -scheme iOSMessup -destination 'id=<simulator-id>' build

# test
xcodebuild -project iOSMessup.xcodeproj -scheme iOSMessupTests -destination 'id=<simulator-id>' test
